## About This Course

Finance teams are being handed Copilot without being told where it may be trusted. This course
closes that gap. You will use Copilot in Word, PowerPoint and Excel on real finance work, build
grounded agents on a governed SharePoint site, automate a finance process in Copilot Studio behind
a human approval gate, orchestrate a multi-agent month-end close, and then decide — with evidence —
whether any of it should be published.

It is written for finance, accounting, treasury, risk and fintech professionals who will be
accountable for the output. Every lab runs on a real Microsoft 365 tenant with synthetic Northstar
Finance data, and every figure across the deck and the labs reconciles to the same
control total.

## Course Topics & Labs

| Topic | Focus | Labs |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Generative AI, Agentic AI and AI Agents in Finance** | How the three differ and why the controls differ with them; finance and fintech use cases for each; Copilot in Word and PowerPoint; building a no-code Microsoft 365 agent | Lab 1 · Lab 2 |
| **2. Excel Copilot for Financial Analysis and Dashboards** | Data readiness, auditing generated formulas, variance analysis, visualisation, dashboards and driver-based forecasting | Lab 4 · Lab 5 |
| **3. SharePoint for Finance and Grounded Finance Agents** | Finance sites, typed lists, approved libraries, permissions, and agents grounded on approved content that refuse rather than guess | Lab 7 |
| **4. Copilot Studio Workflows and Agents for Finance Automation** | A dedicated environment, agent flows, skills and tools, SharePoint connections, and a blocking human approval gate that fails safe | Lab 9 |
| **5. Multi-Agent Orchestration of Financial Work Processes** | Supervisor and specialist agents, connected agents, handoff contracts, month-end close orchestration and where orchestration breaks | Lab 11 |
| **6. AI Security, PDPA and Human Oversight in Finance** | PDPA obligations, data classification, leakage and ownership, Copilot Studio security settings, and oversight that is real rather than decorative | Lab 12 |

Each lab has its own folder with a starter workbook, a solution workbook, a step-by-step README
and an evidence checklist. The two classroom days run the labs listed above; the remaining labs
(3, 6, 8, 10) ship in full as self-paced extension work.
